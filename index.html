A Warm Welcome

You are among family here.

Whether you were born in Australia and are reconnecting with your roots, or you arrived carrying the memories of a homeland left behind — you are welcome here. Assyrian Aid Relief Australia exists because our people have always cared for one another, across borders, across generations, and across every challenge history has placed before us.

This is a place of action, of solidarity, and of deep respect for who we are as people. Together, we can ensure that the Assyrian story — one of extraordinary resilience and enduring culture — continues to be written with dignity, compassion, and hope.

We are glad you are here.

Who We Are

An Australian charity
with Assyrian heart.

Assyrian Aid Relief Australia (AAR) is an Australian-based charity supporting Assyrian families and communities to continue their way of life in our homeland Assyria, also known as Iraq. We exist because our people deserve dignified, effective support that comes from within our own community.

AAR was founded on a simple but powerful belief: that Assyrian-led aid is more than just aid. It is an act of cultural preservation. It is a statement that our people — one of the world's oldest continuous civilisations — will not be forgotten, will not be abandoned, and will not disappear.

We are rooted in Australia, but our reach extends to where our people need us most. Through fundraising events, community gatherings, donation campaigns, and a direct pipeline to on-the-ground support in Iraq, we translate the compassion of the Australian Assyrian community into real, lasting change.

Our Mission

Assyrian Aid Relief Australia exists to support Assyrian communities in Iraq through culturally grounded fundraising, regular giving, and meaningful community engagement. We unite our diaspora to preserve our heritage, strengthen community connections, and fund vital projects that uplift Assyrian families with dignity and hope.

Our Vision

We envision a strong, connected global Assyrian community that actively supports the wellbeing, resilience, and future of Assyrians in Iraq. Through sustained giving, cultural pride, and united action, we aim to build a long‑term platform that empowers our people and preserves our heritage for generations to come.

Our History

Founded as a small, community‑driven initiative, Assyrian Aid Relief Australia was created to provide a reliable and transparent way for Assyrians in Australia to support their homeland. Guided by a volunteer management committee, the organisation has grown through events, outreach, and the collective commitment of community members who share a deep sense of cultural responsibility.

Our Programs

What we do

Our work spans humanitarian aid, Assyrian education, healthcare, agriculture, livelihood, construction, cultural preservation, grassroots fundraising and community engagement — all driven by the Assyrian community, for the Assyrian community.

Humanitarian Relief

Providing direct support to Assyrian families facing displacement, poverty, and crisis — with essential aid that reaches communities in real need, particularly in Iraq.

Community Support

Connecting displaced and vulnerable Assyrian individuals with support networks, practical resources, and the warmth of a community that understands their journey.

Cultural Preservation

Supporting initiatives that keep Assyrian language, traditions, art, and heritage alive — because cultural continuity is inseparable from the well-being of our people.

Fundraising Events

Organising community gatherings, cultural events, and fundraising campaigns that bring Assyrians together and turn collective goodwill into tangible humanitarian impact.

Transparent Reporting

Maintaining clear, honest communication with our donors and community — so you always know exactly how your generosity is being used and what difference it is making.

Volunteer Mobilisation

Building a dedicated network of Assyrian volunteers — elders who carry deep wisdom, and young Assyrians who are rediscovering their identity through service to their community.

Our Partnership

A bridge between
two charities.

One of the defining strengths of Assyrian Aid Relief Australia is our close working relationship with Assyrian Aid Society Iraq — a partnership that transforms donations raised here in Australia into direct, verifiable humanitarian action on the ground in Iraq.

This is not a formal arrangement at arm's length. It is a genuine, community-to-community partnership built on shared identity, mutual trust, and a unified commitment to the welfare of Assyrian people wherever they are.

Because our partner organisation operates within Iraq — embedded in the very communities we serve — we are able to ensure that support reaches families with speed, precision, and cultural sensitivity. We know who receives help. We know how it is used. And we can show you.

This partnership is what makes AAR uniquely positioned as a channel for Assyrian philanthropy. When you donate through AAR, your generosity flows through trusted Assyrian hands, every step of the way.

Where Your Money Goes

95 cents
in every dollar.

Assyrian Aid Relief Australia is run almost entirely by volunteers. Ninety-five percent of every donation goes directly to funded projects on the ground in Assyria, with the remaining five percent covering unavoidable administration — banking and payment fees, insurance, and compliance.

Get Involved

Your community
needs you.

Volunteering with Assyrian Aid Relief Australia is one of the most meaningful ways to live out your Assyrian identity. Whether you have decades of experience or are just beginning to reconnect with your heritage, there is a place for you here.

For our elders

Carry your wisdom forward.

You have lived through history that younger generations can only read about. Your experience, your language, your stories — these are irreplaceable gifts to our community. As a volunteer, your wisdom shapes how we work, who we reach, and what we stand for. We need your guidance, your cultural knowledge, and your steady presence.

For younger Assyrians

Reclaim your roots through service.

If you are a young Assyrian who feels the pull of your heritage but isn't sure how to honour it — this is your invitation. Volunteering with AAR is how you turn that feeling into action. You will work alongside people who share your background, contribute to causes that matter deeply, and discover what it means to be Assyrian through the most powerful lens there is: giving back.

Healthcare Mobile Clinic — Medical Care for People in Need in Duhok Governorate
In Progress
Duhok Governorate, Iraq
Rural and underserved areas face limited access to health centres, diagnostic services and essential medications. Through its mobile clinic, the Assyrian Aid Society provides basic healthcare services and essential medicines to people in need in remote villages. This project will support half a month of mobile clinic activities, providing medical care and necessary medications to underserved communities in Duhok Governorate.
